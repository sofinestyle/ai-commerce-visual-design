# Project Overview

## Product Name

AI 电商视觉设计平台

## Why This Software Exists

E-commerce visual production is often fragmented across product spreadsheets, asset folders, design tools, prompt experiments, review comments, and final exports. Teams lose time switching contexts, repeating manual layout decisions, and trying to remember which asset, prompt, or version produced a final image.

AI 电商视觉设计平台 exists to turn that scattered process into a structured visual workflow. It gives teams a single desktop workspace for product context, media assets, project organization, AI-assisted generation, review, export, and history.

## Target Users

- E-commerce operators who need campaign-ready product visuals.
- Designers who need a controlled workspace for AI-assisted image production.
- Brand teams who need visual consistency across products and platforms.
- Content teams who need reusable workflows for product launches.
- Small businesses that want AI help without losing human review.

## Product Vision

The product vision is a professional, local-first Web application where AI helps users produce better e-commerce visuals faster, while the user remains in control of creative direction and final decisions.

The application should feel like an enterprise SaaS workspace: structured, calm, reliable, and optimized for repeated daily work.

## Long-Term Goal

The long-term goal is to become a complete visual production operating system for e-commerce teams. The platform should eventually support product intake, source media management, prompt workflows, model selection, image generation, comparison, editing, export presets, and traceable production history.

The product starts local-first and desktop-only, but should remain cloud-ready for future collaboration, account systems, team workspaces, and external integrations.

## Core Philosophy

- AI assists, humans decide.
- Workflow matters more than isolated generation.
- Product context should guide creative output.
- Documentation is part of the product, not an afterthought.
- Reusable systems should come before one-off screens.
- Local-first development keeps the early product fast, inspectable, and controllable.
